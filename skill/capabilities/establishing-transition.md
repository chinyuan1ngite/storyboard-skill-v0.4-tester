# Establishing and Transition Capability

Status: `FORMALIZED_PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`.

Load when the audience must learn a time/place relation or follow a meaningful move between spaces.

- Establish only spatial facts later action needs: regions, connections, landmarks, entry/exit, and travel direction.
- Let movement demonstrate relation when clearer than a static geography inventory.
- Show sufficient departure/arrival evidence to prevent an unsupported jump; connected spaces need no redundant wide Shot.
- Preserve script time and location; do not add events to animate the environment.
- One Shot is valid when it establishes relation and movement legibly.

Does not own production-design prose, atmosphere syntax, complete maps, or establishing-Shot quotas.
