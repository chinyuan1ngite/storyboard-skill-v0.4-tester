# Dialogue Capability

Status: `FORMALIZED_PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`.

Load when spoken exchange or a single speaker/listener relationship is dominant.

- Preserve every scripted line exactly and bind it to the correct speaker.
- Decide per line whether the speaker is on-screen, off-screen, or crosses the cut.
- Show necessary listener reception when it changes meaning; a separate reaction Shot is not mandatory when the same frame is readable.
- Maintain dialogue axis and eyeline unless a motivated re-establishing relation makes a side change unambiguous.
- Cut on changed information, power, reaction, or speaking/listening function—not mechanically on every line.
- Use framing and duration that can show required mouth movement or listening behavior.

Does not own group topology, action choreography, general continuity, cross-Shot audio syntax, prompt wording, QA, or workflow.
