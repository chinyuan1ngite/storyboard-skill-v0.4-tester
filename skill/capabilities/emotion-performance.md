# Emotion and Performance Capability

Status: `FORMALIZED_PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`.

Load when an observable internal change or performance reaction is the primary result.

- Bind emotion to its stimulus and preserve perception, pause, bodily/facial change, and resulting action order.
- Choose framing by the smallest cue the audience must read; do not force a close-up when posture or relationship is the evidence.
- Give performance enough uninterrupted duration without invented padding.
- Distinguish simultaneous mixed emotions from sequential change.
- Keep an off-screen stimulus off-screen while the script withholds it.

Does not own invented backstory, beauty-shot construction, reaction quotas, cross-Shot audio, or reveal order.
