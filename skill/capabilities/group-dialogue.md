# Group Dialogue Capability

Status: `FORMALIZED_PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`.

Load when three or more active participants, interruption, or attention transfer is dominant.

- Register each active participant and keep speaker, listener, and attention target unambiguous.
- Establish the minimum topology before a reaction or line depends on it; do not re-establish unchanged space repeatedly.
- Make interruption/entry readable as stimulus, group transfer, then new center.
- Keep screen direction and eyelines coherent across singles, pairs, and group frames.
- Preserve simultaneous group reactions when the script requires multiple receivers.
- Split only when legibility, timing, or information control requires it.

Does not own generic source coverage, transition geography, or full 3D blocking.
